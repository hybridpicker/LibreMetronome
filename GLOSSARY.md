# Glossary

**First beat**: The first click after the player presses play. It is always beat 1 of the bar, it is always audible, and nothing may be shown before it sounds. LibreMetronome has no count-in.

**Sound set**: A named group of click sounds (accent, normal beat, subdivision) that the player picks in the settings. Exactly one sound set is active at a time.

**Bundled clicks**: The default click sounds that ship inside the app. They play when the active sound set is not available yet or fails to load.

**Beat indicator**: Everything on screen that shows the beat: the pendulum in analog mode, the highlighted beat in circle mode, the active cell in grid mode. It follows the same clock as the audio, so it never runs ahead of the sound.
