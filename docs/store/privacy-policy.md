## The LibreMetronome app (Android, later iOS)

The app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

- It has no internet permission and makes no network requests.
- It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking and contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.
- Your settings (tempo, volume, time signature, display and accessibility options, chosen sound) are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them. We cannot see them.
- Purchase and payment are handled entirely by Google Play. We receive no payment data from you. Google's own privacy policy applies to the purchase and to refunds: https://policies.google.com/privacy
